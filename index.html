Planned programs

From a meal to a livelihood

These food and livelihood programs for Karachi are in the planning stage. Activities will begin after we receive donations.

Free meals · Muft Dastarkhwan

What’s planned
Serve free, cooked meals at a community dastarkhwan.
Who it’s for
People who struggle to afford a daily meal.

Ration & food packs

What’s planned
Distribute grocery staples and food packs for meals at home.
Who it’s for
Families in need, widows, orphans and daily-wage workers.

Emergency food relief

What’s planned
Set up food relief camps during floods, disasters and other emergencies.
Who it’s for
People and families who need immediate access to food after an emergency.

Surplus food recovery

What’s planned
Collect safe surplus meals from hotels and wedding halls and raise public awareness of hunger and food waste.
Who it’s for
Individuals and households who need a meal.

Skills & livelihood

What’s planned
Offer small-scale skills training and provide motorcycle and scooter support for earning a living.
Who it’s for
People out of work who need a practical skill or transport to access earning opportunities.

Governance & transparency

Our founding documents.

The foundation’s documents set out its objectives, how funds are used and who is responsible for running it.

Memorandum of Association

Name and objectives

The memorandum gives the organization’s name as Rizq-e-Rawan Welfare and lists its registered office at the Karachi address above.

  1. Distribute cooked meals, rations and food packs to widows, orphans, daily-wage workers and others in need.
  2. Set up food relief camps for people affected by natural disasters, floods and other emergencies.
  3. Raise awareness of hunger and food waste, and collect safe surplus food from hotels and wedding halls for people in need.
  4. Run small-scale skills training programs to help unemployed people find work.
  5. Operate on a non-political, non-religious and nonprofit basis.
Use of income and donations

All income, donations, zakat and contributions are reserved for the stated objectives. Members receive no share of profits.

Members and contact

The memorandum calls for at least three members and lists the Karachi office contact shown above.

Articles of Association

Read the short articles
  1. Membership is open to anyone who agrees with the foundation’s objectives.
  2. The General Body meets at least once a year.
  3. The President is responsible for the foundation’s financial and administrative affairs.
  4. The bank account requires joint signatures from the President and either the General Secretary or the Treasurer.
